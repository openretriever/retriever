# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be respectful. Participation in Retriever should be a harassment-free experience
for everyone.

**Welcome:** empathy and kindness, respect for differing views, graceful
feedback, and focusing on what's best for the community.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualized
language or attention, publishing someone's private information, or anything else
you wouldn't do in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

All project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and Discord — and
anywhere you're representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers (see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Reports are handled confidentially and reviewed
promptly.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this Code, and
may warn or ban anyone whose behavior warrants it.
